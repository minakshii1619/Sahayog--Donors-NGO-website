How it works

Every NGO on Sahayog goes through the same review.

A verification badge means our team has checked the onboarding information an NGO submitted — not that it's government-certified.

1

NGO registers & submits documents

Registration number, NGO-DARPAN ID, address, bank details, and a description of their work.

2

Admin reviews the application

Our team checks the submitted information before approving the organisation to go live.

3

NGO publishes profiles & campaigns

Once approved, the NGO can describe its work and open specific campaigns for donors to support.

4

Donors find, give, and get a receipt

Search by cause or city, choose a campaign, donate, and receive a digital confirmation for your records.
